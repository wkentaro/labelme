Fixed a selected label in the Annotation List rendering white-on-pale-gray, and so being unreadable, whenever the list did not hold keyboard focus (most commonly right after selecting a shape on the canvas, which leaves focus on the canvas). The custom HTML delegate bypassed Qt's text renderer and hardcoded the palette's `Active` group. Labels are now stored as plain text and painted through Qt's stock item-view style, and the delegate itself draws only the trailing label-color dot. This preserves the dot's position after the label and lets Qt handle focused, unfocused, disabled, light-theme, and dark-theme text colors
